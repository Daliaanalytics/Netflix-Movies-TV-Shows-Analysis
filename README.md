# Netflix-Movies-TV-Shows-Analysis

## Project Overview
Analysis of 8,800 Netflix titles including movies and TV shows,
identifying content trends, top producing countries, ratings distribution,
and popular genres.

## Tools Used
- Python (Pandas, Matplotlib, SQLAlchemy) — Data Cleaning & EDA
- SQL Server (SSMS) — Database & Queries
- Power BI — Dashboard

## SQL Analysis
- Movies vs TV Shows Distribution
- Top 10 Countries by Content Production
- Content Growth by Year (2010-2021)
- Content by Age Rating
- Top 10 Genres

## Dashboard Pages
- **Page 1 — Overview:** Total Content, Movies, TV Shows, Distribution, Top Countries
- **Page 2 — Analysis:** Ratings, Top Genres, Content by Year

## Key Findings
- **69%** of Netflix content are Movies, **30%** are TV Shows
- **United States** is the top producer with **2,815** titles
- **India** is second with **972** titles (Bollywood!)
- **Egypt** is in top 10 with **106** titles
- Content peaked in **2018** with **1,147** titles
- **TV-MA** is the most common rating at **36%**
- **Documentaries** is the most popular genre
